Colombo Central Hub

Colombo General Post Office (GPO) & Central Mail Exchange

Located at the commercial heart of Colombo near Fort Railway Station and Lake House, the Postal Headquarters handles national routing and provides round-the-clock counter services.

01
24/7 Continuous Counter

Operating 24 hours a day, 365 days a year for urgent registered articles, airmail parcels, and stamp franking.

02
Transit Accessibility

Only 400 meters from Colombo Fort Railway Station and Bastian Mawatha Central Bus Terminal.

03
Philatelic Sales Counter

First day covers, commemorative stamps, and collectors' albums available on the ground floor foyer.
