Postal Security & Safety

Prohibited & Restricted Articles

In compliance with Universal Postal Union and Sri Lanka Customs regulations, hazardous and restricted items are governed strictly.

Strictly Prohibited Items

Post Office Ordinance Sec. 16 — Absolute Ban

The following items must never be consigned under any circumstance. Detection leads to immediate confiscation and law enforcement referral:

  • Explosives, ammunition, fireworks & flares: Any detonating, combustible, or pyrotechnic chemical compound.
  • Flammable gases, liquids & solids: Matches, lighters, aerosols, petrol, paints, solvents, kerosene, and alcohol over 70% ABV.
  • Narcotics, psychotropic drugs & illicit substances: Controlled pharmaceuticals without authorized government licenses.
  • Currency notes, bullion & bearer securities: Physical currency notes, coins, gold bullion, and traveler cheques via standard mail.
  • Live animals, reptiles & infectious organisms: Pathological cultures, venomous insects, biohazard substances, and rabies vectors.
  • Obscene, offensive, or subversive media: Materials that violate national sovereignty or public morality statutes.

Conditionally Restricted Items

Acceptable Only with Certified Permits & Packaging

These articles are accepted exclusively when accompanied by statutory permits and compliant specialized protective packaging:

  • Lithium-ion Batteries (UN 3481): Permitted ONLY when permanently installed inside consumer electronics (max 2 spare cells with certified UN packaging labels).
  • Pharmaceutical Prescription Medicines: Allowed when accompanied by an authorized Sri Lanka National Medicines Regulatory Authority (NMRA) physician certificate.
  • Perfumes, Cosmetics & Essential Oils: Permitted in domestic mail up to 100 ml per vial with hermetically sealed leak-proof absorbent layers.
  • Commercial Foodstuffs & Spices: Dry, non-perishable agricultural produce with Plant Quarantine Certification from the Department of Agriculture.
  • Perishable Biological Specimens: Authorized academic and medical laboratory diagnostic samples utilizing dry-ice triple packaging.
  • Gems, Jewellery & Valuables: Permitted ONLY under Insured Letter / Parcel Post service with declared value stamps.

Packaging & Dimension Standards

Adhere to standardized packaging to prevent transit damage and automated sorting rejection.

01

Minimum Dimensions

Letters and packets must measure no less than 90 mm × 140 mm with a tolerance of 2 mm for machine barcoding.

02

Maximum Parcel Size

Maximum length 1.5 meters; length plus greatest circumference measured in another direction must not exceed 3 meters.

03

Cushioning & Void Fill

Fragile items require 50 mm of bubble wrap or high-density foam on all 6 sides, with no free movement inside outer carton.

04

Clear Recipient Labelling

Write recipient name, complete street address, town, and 5-digit postal code in clear Latin or national script on the lower right face.

RTI Request Procedure

Follow these straightforward statutory steps to request departmental documents and public information:

  1. 1

    Submit Form RTI 01

    Obtain and fill Form RTI 01 specifying clearly the information, records, or statistics you wish to access.

  2. 2

    Formal Acknowledgment (3 Days)

    The Information Officer will issue written acknowledgment within 3 working days of submission.

  3. 3

    Decision & Fulfillment (14 Days)

    A decision is rendered within the statutory limit of 14 working days, with fees charged strictly according to RTI Commission gazettes.

  4. 4

    Statutory Appeal Rights

    If information is denied or delayed, file an appeal to the Designated Officer (PMG) within 14 days of decision.

Citizen Delivery Charter

Delivery targets and customer service commitments across the island network.

24 Hours

Intra-City Delivery

Ordinary letters and registered packets posted before 11:00 AM are delivered within the same municipality or metropolitan area within 24 Hours.

48 Hours

Inter-Provincial Mail

Mail traversing between administrative provinces across major arterial highways is sorted overnight and delivered within 48 Hours.

72 Hours

Rural & Remote Delivery

Sub post offices in estate sectors, agricultural colonies, and remote island regions are served with scheduled bi-weekly or 72-Hour cycles.

Next Day AM

EMS Speed Post Courier

Dedicated courier vehicles ensure next morning door-to-door delivery between all 25 district secretariats and priority commercial hubs.
