About Us

Serving the nation since 1798.

Vision & Mission Our Insights Major Functions

MISSION

To provide a highly innovative, efficient and reliable postal service utilizing motivated staff and modern technology in a pleasant work environment to maximize customer satisfaction while adhering to the standards of the Universal Postal Union and upholding the policies of the Government of Sri Lanka.

VISION

Become the partner of excellence in relations, by providing innovative and diverse service.

Major functions

How Sri Lanka Post delivers on its mission.

  • Provision of an efficient and reliable service in tandem with all stakeholders as a team under any environmental and social condition, under the guidance of the leadership and the promotion of income.
  • Converting the postal service, which has earned the confidence of the consumer by providing a door-to-door service, into a more qualitative and efficient service by employing modern marketing strategies.
  • Making the consumer delighted by offering services in a pleasing and attractive office environment.
  • With the dawn of peace, promoting postal services by effectively deploying the skilled workforce in communication and social welfare activities.
  • Formulation and implementation of a strategic plan focused on the new technological market that advances the cause of the postal service in collaboration with the relevant stakeholders.
  • Achieving institutional improvement by the optimum use of technology with local and foreign assistance.
  • Enhancing the quality and effectiveness of services provided by making use of rapidly developing infrastructure facilities.
  • Introducing a viable and transparent technology-based audit and investigation system agreeable to all.
  • Bringing about an attitudinal change for the provision of a dignified service, updating intrinsic value systems of Sri Lanka Post as a flagship communication entity of the state sector.
  • Deploying disciplined staff empowered by continuous training and a system of methodical performance appraisal in such a way as to consolidate public trust.
  • Motivating the staff by implementing a methodical promotion procedure and transfer system for the provision of quality and efficient consumer service.
  • Gainfully employing the creative contribution of trade unions for the betterment of the institution and establishing industrial peace.
  • Becoming the best postal hub of South Asia by providing a more efficient service through proper study of work, income-based grading and employee motivation.
  • Making the post office network a commercial centre providing multi-purpose services that can attract tourists and win public trust.
  • Efficiently employing the support services, making timely and forthright decisions with the relevant parties, while innovatively absorbing the advantages of the rapidly growing advertising market.
  • Expanding the postal service to satisfy the needs of Sri Lankans living abroad.
  • Taking Sri Lankan identity to the international arena by propagating the hobby of philately within the country and overseas.
  • Sri Lanka Post, which holds membership of the Universal Postal Union, has won public trust and is equipped with an island-wide network, conquering the technology-based competitive market.
  • Formulating laws that ensure the monopoly in the delivery of letters and give a competitive edge and efficient acceptance, transportation and distribution of postal items.
  • Gaining competitive advantage by reorganising the organisational structure and formulating the relevant laws.
